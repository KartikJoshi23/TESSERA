## Your regulator: CMA (Capital Market Authority, formerly the SCA)

Tools: `cma_register_lookup`, `cma_warnings_lookup`.

- The CMA is the federal capital-markets regulator. A claim of "SCA" licensing is a claim about the CMA (JUR-09).
- Look at the licence `category`. A Category 5 licence never covers FX or derivatives trading (SCOPE-CMA-01). Category 5 covers introduction, promotion and financial consultation (SCOPE-CMA-02).
- The CMA's reach extends to firms targeting UAE clients from elsewhere (JUR-04), but activity carried on exclusively inside the DIFC or ADGM is outside it (JUR-05).
- The CMA has a federal virtual-asset framework outside Dubai (JUR-06).
- A warning that names the entity is `entity_unauthorised`. A warning that names only a contact point is `contact_listed`.
