# Draft: request to the DFSA for written permission

Send this from your own email, using the contact route listed on the DFSA homepage
(`https://www.dfsa.ae/`). Edit the bracketed parts first. Sending it costs nothing, and a written
answer is the strongest basis for any later publication.

---

**Subject:** Request for permission: limited use of public register and alert facts in a
non-commercial research project

Dear DFSA team,

I am [name], [role, institution if any], working on a non-commercial research project that
studies how software can help residents check whether an investment offer matches official
regulatory records. The UAE Ministry of Interior has advised residents to verify licences with
the relevant authorities before investing, and our work evaluates tools that support that check.

Your Legal Disclaimer limits extracts to personal use or use within an organisation, and asks
for written permission before material is stored in any electronic retrieval system. We would
therefore like to ask for permission for the following limited use:

1. A person on our team would record, by hand, a small number of facts from your public register
   and 2026 alerts: firm names, Firm Reference Numbers, authorisation status, permitted services,
   alert dates, and the domains or contact details named in alerts. This would cover about
   [number] firms and [number] alerts.
2. The facts would be kept in one access-restricted file on a single computer, used only to
   evaluate our research software. They would never be indexed, published, shared or used in a
   public tool. No pages, screenshots or text would be copied.
3. The software never accesses your website automatically, and any link to the DFSA points only
   to your homepage.
4. Published results would report only aggregate measures, such as accuracy rates, without
   reproducing your material.

We would be grateful to know whether this use is acceptable, and whether you would prefer any
other conditions. We are also happy to share our results with you.

Kind regards,
